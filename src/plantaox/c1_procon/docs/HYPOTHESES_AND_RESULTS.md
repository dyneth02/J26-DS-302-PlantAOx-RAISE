## Reproducibility note
Training is deterministic across machines (Colab torch 2.11 vs local torch 2.9: identical checkpoints and prototype distances). The probe AUROCs differ by about ±0.03 between two environments (scikit-learn 1.6.1 vs 1.7.2 assign groups to folds differently): **probe AUROCs are only meaningful to roughly ±0.03**, and the conclusions drawn above (ESM-2 ≈ composition, leakage inflation, no scale benefit) hold in both runs. Retrieval ΔAP values agree to the third decimal; Recall@5 of near-collapsed models can shift by a few points because their similarities differ only in the last digits.

## What can and cannot be claimed
* **Can:** a leak-controlled multi-label benchmark (S2 v3, homology groups, strict / extended / ≥ 4 aa modes); a measured leakage inflation (+0.02 … +0.19 AUROC); a retrieval protocol shown to be saturated and corrected; an honest negative result (contrastive projection < untrained baselines).
* **Cannot:** that the contrastive model works; that mechanism-aware pairing helps; that ESM-2 encodes FRS/MC beyond composition; the earlier "AUROC ≈ 0.8, homology-safe" and "0.54 → 0.83" numbers.
* **Limits:** seeds change only initialisation and batch order (not the split); two fold directions; n = 49 queries (55 in the reverse direction), no external validation set, noisy assay-level labels, strict-mode MC training signal comes from dual peptides only (all 18 strict MC-only peptides are in the query fold).
