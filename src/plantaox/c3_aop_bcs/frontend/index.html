02 / Research gap

A high score is not enough.

Conventional validation asks whether predictions are correct. AOP-BCS adds a second question: are they behaviourally credible under biologically motivated edits?

A reliable predictor should react to redox-disrupting edits, remain stable under conservative edits, and behave differently from random controls.
