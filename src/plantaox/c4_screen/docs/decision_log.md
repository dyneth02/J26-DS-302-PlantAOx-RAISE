# Decision log

## Component 4 — candidate generation

- **Proteome scope:** use UniProt Swiss-Prot reviewed entries selected by taxonomy ID. The proposal's proteome identifiers include unreviewed entries, so reviewed-only counts and exclusions are recorded separately.
- **Digestion implementation:** use the installed `pyteomics` Expasy-style rules rather than a BIOPEP-UWM batch workflow. Installed `pyteomics 5.0.1` provides the exact rule names `pepsin ph1.3` and `trypsin`; pepsin pH 1.3 is the nearest available rule to proposed pH 1.2.
- **Deduplication:** exact sequence equality within and across plants; preserve every parent accession and 1-based inclusive position. CD-HIT is deferred. At 90% identity, peptides up to 9 residues cannot have a non-identical sequence meeting the threshold; optional clustering for lengths 10–20 remains a later decision.
- **Signals:** signal inputs remain mock-only and must retain `source="mock"`. The config's BCS flag thresholds are explicitly `C4-provisional`; this Gate 1 does not generate or evaluate signals.

## Component 4 — embeddings, AD, and mock integration (19 October 2026)

- **Embedding recipe:** the supplied manifest verifies `esm2_t33_650M_UR50D`, layer 33, mean pooling over residue embeddings excluding BOS/EOS/PAD, float16 storage, 1280 dimensions, N=152,259, and Colab device Tesla T4. This remains a **C4 working assumption**, not confirmed by C1. Embeddings were imported; no local ESM-2 weights were loaded or downloaded.
- **Reference cloud:** the supplied 500-sequence cloud is a composition-biased synthetic placeholder with seed 42 and source `mock_cloud`. It is not a biological reference set; every derived AD output is marked MOCK. C4 did not generate or embed a replacement cloud.
- **AD calibration:** `d_AD = 1 - cosine(x, centroid)`; calibrate `mu_cloud` and population `sigma_cloud` on leave-one-out distances, where each cloud member is compared with a centroid recomputed without that member. Observed `mu_cloud=0.0185099002`, `sigma_cloud=0.0097886926`, `t1=0.0282985928`, `t2=0.0380872854` with multiplier 1.0. Normalize with `d_norm=min(d_AD/t2,1)`. These values are calibrated against the MOCK cloud, not biological positives.
- **Diagnostics and sensitivity:** Spearman correlations between `d_AD` and sequence length and MOCK-cloud/candidate length distributions are descriptive only. No plant exceeded the >95% single-tier degeneracy criterion (maximum rice Tier 1 = 56.99%). A requested sensitivity check was nevertheless run separately: subtract the joint feature-wise mean of all candidate and MOCK-cloud vectors, then recompute the MOCK cloud centroid, leave-one-out calibration, and tier fractions using the unchanged calibration rule. Sensitivity `mu=0.4238381`, `sigma=0.2141557`, `t1=0.6379938`, `t2=0.8521495`; across all candidates, Tier 1/2/3 fractions changed from 57.54/25.23/17.23% to 9.41/18.87/71.73%. This is a labelled sensitivity result only; production tiers, thresholds, and `ad_params.json` were not changed, and no threshold tuning was performed.
- **Signal and score contract:** use the existing `tests/c4_screen/fixtures/mock_signals.py`; join by sequence, retain `source="mock"`, score with configured 0.5/0.5 weights, and force Tier 3 CS to zero. Never report PARRS, Recall@K, MRR, EF@1%, or ARR for mock signals.
- **PDSS:** the denominator/baseline can be near zero and make PDSS unstable. This remains an open supervisor decision; no PDSS is implemented or reported here.
